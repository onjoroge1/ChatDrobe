ChatDrobe curated world artwork

Selected vector components derive from these collections by DiceBear:
- Sprouts by DiceBear: two potted plant compositions for Tokyo.
  https://www.dicebear.com/styles/sprouts/
- Landscape by DiceBear: three hill contours for Train.
  https://www.dicebear.com/styles/landscape/
- Planets by DiceBear: terra, cratered and banded surfaces, and soft shading for Starship.
  https://www.dicebear.com/styles/planets/

animation/randomization, mapped selected colors to existing lighting palettes,
namespaced SVG references, and composed the art in its existing world stages.
